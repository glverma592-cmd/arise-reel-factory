# Research — reel ka content dhoondhna

Goal: 30–60 second ki reel ke liye **sach, kaam ka, aur surprising** material.

1. Topic ko ek sawaal me badlo jo viewer ke mann me hai.
   Example: "AI resume" → "Main AI se apna resume jaldi aur achha kaise banaun?"
2. 3–6 web searches karo (latest info, official sources, numbers). Purane ya bina source wale numbers mat lo.
3. Ye cheezein nikaalo:
   - **1 hook fact** — chaunkane wala number ya common galti
   - **3–5 main points / steps** — har point ek line me samajh aaye
   - **1 example** — before/after, ya ek real use case
   - **1 warning / mistake** (optional)
4. `projects/<slug>/research.md` me likho:

```markdown
# <Topic>
## Hook ideas
- ...
## Key points
- ...
## Numbers (with source)
- 7 sec — recruiters ka average resume scan time — <source URL>
## Sources
- <title> — <URL>
```

Rule: agar koi number confirm nahi hota, use script me mat daalo.
